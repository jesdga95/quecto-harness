Keep a short plan for the work in hand.
Send the whole plan each time; it replaces what was there. A plan with every step completed is cleared when the next turn starts.

Each step has content and a status: pending, in_progress, or completed.
Mark one step in_progress at a time, and completed before starting the next.
List the completed steps first, then the one in progress, then the pending ones. To take a step out of order, move it to its place in the list.
A call with an unknown key or status, an empty step, a repeated one or a list out of that order is refused and changes nothing.
Use for work spanning several steps, not a single command or edit.
