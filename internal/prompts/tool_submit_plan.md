Send the plan for the user to approve, and wait for the answer: approved, or what to change.

title: the change, in a few words.
goal: what the change is for and the approach taken, in one or two sentences.
files: each file to change, with the functions or sections in it and what changes there.
steps: the work in order, one line each, naming its files. They become the todo list.
check: the tests to add or change, the commands to run, and what to look at by hand.
open: what is still undecided; leave it empty when nothing is.
